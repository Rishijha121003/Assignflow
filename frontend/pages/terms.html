2. Account Registration and Security

  • Account Integrity: Users must register with valid, authentic institutional email addresses. You are responsible for all activities conducted under your user account.
  • Role Boundaries: Users must register under their legitimate academic role (Teacher or Student). Impersonating an instructor, using false credentials, or attempting to register as an instructor without authorization is strictly prohibited.
  • Credential Confidentiality: You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password. You must notify the administrator immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

3. Coursework Submissions and Academic Integrity

  • Academic Honesty: All coursework, code, essays, and files submitted through AssignFlow must represent the student's original academic work, created in accordance with course instructions and the institution's academic integrity policies.
  • File Formats & Limits: Uploaded submission files must strictly comply with supported file extensions (.pdf, .docx, .doc, .txt, .zip) and must not exceed the maximum file size limit (10MB). Submissions containing non-conforming or unparseable formats may be rejected by system validation.
  • Submission Deadlines & Policies: Coursework submissions are subject to deadlines configured by the instructor. Assignments configured to reject late work will automatically reject attempts after the specified due date and grace period. Late submissions, where permitted by policy, are subject to automated deduction percentages set by the instructor.
  • Audit Logging: All submissions, resubmission attempts, timestamped receipts, and file replacements are recorded in immutable audit logs accessible to course instructors.

4. Instructor Authority and Evaluation

  • Course Administration: Instructors have full authority to create classes, issue enrollment codes, publish assignments, set submission policies, extend deadlines, and evaluate student work.
  • Grading & Grade Release: Evaluative marks, rubrics, and feedback entered by instructors remain drafts until explicitly released by the instructor. Once released, grades are finalized in accordance with course syllabus rules.
  • Re-evaluation & Corrections: Students may submit re-evaluation requests through the platform subject to instructor review. Instructors retain final authority on all grade adjustments and corrections, which are recorded in the system audit log.

5. Prohibited Activities

Users of AssignFlow must strictly refrain from any of the following activities:

  • Uploading executable files, scripts, viruses, malware, or corrupted archives designed to compromise server infrastructure or peer devices.
  • Attempting directory traversal, SQL injection, CSV formula manipulation, or unauthorized API bypass to access other students' files or instructor grading dashboards.
  • Sharing course join codes or assignment materials with unauthorized individuals outside the enrolled class.
  • Running automated scrapers, denial-of-service tools, or load-testing scripts against the application without prior written authorization from the system administrator.

6. Service Availability and Disclaimers

AssignFlow is provided to the academic community on an "as is" and "as available" basis. While system architecture includes automated health checks, database connection recovery, and transactional data integrity safeguards, the platform operators do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation.

Students are strongly encouraged to retain local copies of all submitted coursework and to submit assignments well in advance of published deadlines to prevent unexpected network or technical disruptions.

7. Suspension and Termination

The platform administrator reserves the right to suspend or terminate user accounts, revoke course enrollment, or restrict access to the system for any violation of these Terms and Conditions or applicable institutional policies.
